# NEXORA AI — First Customer Acquisition Plan
Version 1.0 · ₹0 budget · India + international

## Goal
Acquire one legitimate paying pilot customer, deliver the limited service accurately, obtain feedback, and improve the process. No income is guaranteed.

## Initial offer
NEXORA Website Security Hygiene Review:
- India pilot price: ₹499 for one website.
- International pilot price: US$9, subject to payment availability, fees, local law and agreed currency.
- One exact URL approved in writing.
- One HTTP HEAD request; no redirect following.
- Review selected response headers and deliver a short report.
- Not a penetration test, comprehensive vulnerability assessment, compliance audit, certification or security guarantee.

Only sell tasks the reviewer can competently perform, manually verify and explain. If uncertain, decline or refer to a qualified professional.

## Customer segments
### India
- Independent consultants and coaches with brochure-style websites.
- Small local service businesses with simple informational websites.
- Freelance web developers and small web-design agencies who may refer clients.
### International
- Independent consultants and coaches.
- Small creative and web-design agencies.
- Portfolio and brochure-site owners.

Avoid high-risk or sensitive targets initially: hospitals, banks, government, payment providers, production systems handling sensitive data, and any system outside the reviewer's competence.

## Free acquisition channels
- LinkedIn: manually identify owners and web developers; personalized messages only.
- Google Maps and business directories: manually review publicly listed business contact channels; do not scrape or automate bulk messages.
- Relevant Facebook groups: participate only where service promotion is allowed.
- Reddit and developer communities: answer questions helpfully and follow each community's self-promotion rules.
- Existing personal/professional network: ask for introductions without pressure.
- Freelance marketplaces: use only genuinely free account features; check platform fees before accepting paid work. Do not spend on bids or subscriptions.

## Daily operating target (first 10 working days)
- Research 5 relevant prospects manually.
- Send up to 5 personalized messages, only through appropriate public business contact channels and in compliance with platform rules and local law.
- Record the contact source, date, message, response and opt-out.
- Send at most one polite follow-up after 4–7 days.
- Stop immediately if the person declines or asks not to be contacted.
- Never claim a vulnerability was found unless a permitted, completed review supports that exact statement.

## Message sequence
1. First contact: explain the limited one-URL header hygiene review, proposed pilot fee and written-authorization requirement.
2. Interested reply: send the scope and authorization template; answer questions honestly.
3. Before payment or testing: agree in writing on scope, fee, delivery date, data handling and cancellation/refund terms.
4. Before test: verify the signed authorization and exact URL, date/time and exclusions.
5. After test: manually validate report, send privately, delete working data on the agreed date.
6. Ask for honest feedback. Ask separately for explicit permission before publishing a testimonial, name, logo or case study.

## 30-day plan
### Week 1 — readiness
- Run unit tests and test the local assistant on the owner's Windows machine.
- Generate a sample report using only an owned lab/site or clearly labeled synthetic sample.
- Prepare a short service explanation and authorization process.
- Do not advertise a full penetration test.
### Week 2 — conversations
- Identify 25 suitable India prospects and 15 international prospects manually.
- Send relevant, personalized messages.
- Track replies and questions; do not mass-message.
### Week 3 — first pilot
- Follow up once where appropriate.
- Confirm client authority and written scope before work.
- Complete only the one-request review and deliver a checked report.
### Week 4 — improve
- Ask for feedback and permission for a testimonial.
- Review time spent, actual costs/fees, conversion and customer satisfaction.
- Update the offer based on what customers actually value.
- Do not scale or claim results based on one pilot.

## Zero-budget controls
- No paid ads, domains, hosting, APIs, scanners, subscriptions, or cloud compute.
- Do not buy leads or scrape contact data.
- Do not promise income, rankings, security guarantees or vulnerability discovery.
- Check payment processor fees, currency conversion, taxes and local requirements before quoting or accepting money. A ₹0 marketing budget does not mean payment processing or tax obligations are zero.
- Never collect client passwords, session cookies, API keys or unrelated personal data.

## Success measures
Primary: one satisfied paying pilot customer.
Track: personalized contacts sent, positive replies, authorization forms completed, paid jobs, delivery time, refunds/complaints and permissioned testimonials.
